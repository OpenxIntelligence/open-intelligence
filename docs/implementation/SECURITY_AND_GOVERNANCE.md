## Dataset admission contract

Maintain one versioned rights record per dataset release **and access tier**, linked to every imported artifact and derived run. The data steward must record the official source, acquisition path, exact release/checksum, data owner, data-use agreement (DUA) and access authority, consent basis and scope where relevant, allowed organization/project/purpose, storage region, expiry, redistribution, display, training and external-provider permissions separately, retention/deletion obligations and accountable owner. Store evidence and review date; a blank/unknown permission is a denial. An open-source client, public accession or accessible paper does not clear the underlying record. Current scope permits independently authored fictional cases, not acquisition of identifiable or restricted cohort records.

| Intake under consideration | Project policy pending exact review |
|---|---|
| Synthea | Evaluate the specific generator, assets and output separately. Use only fixtures with recorded fictional origin and no personal-record derivation; importing a collection is a separate decision. |
| PhysioNet, MIMIC-IV and MIMIC-IV-Note | Evaluate the exact project, release, credentialing, DUA and separately governed note access. Do not acquire records, commit extracts, reuse for training or send to an external provider. |
| TCGA/GDC, GEO and SRA | Classify exact release, accession, file and access tier. Keep controlled data and derived artifacts blocked; do not assume permissions flow from public metadata to participant-level files. |
| ClinVar and CIViC | Check each release, submission/annotation provenance, correction status, data and attribution terms; clinical-significance labels remain attributable assertions. No chart write from a database label. |
| ClinicalTrials.gov and PubMed | Separate registry/bibliographic metadata from eligibility judgments, abstracts and full text. Record retrieval date, version/correction status and field-level rights; no enrollment or unsupported clinical claims. |
| SEER | Identify the exact product and governing agreement, permitted analyses and publication/disclosure conditions before acquisition; never infer permission from access to published aggregate statistics. |

A grant to read a clinical record cannot authorize research enrollment, dataset training or provider egress. A computational output inherits applicable restrictions. Rights correction must stop future use/disclosure, invalidate cached projections and mark dependent results; retain only the allowed audit trace. Deletion procedures must identify copies, exports and backups, specify erasure/expiry or a documented hold, and record what was actually completed. A platform receipt cannot revoke a copy already downloaded.

**Release evidence (OI-004, OI-012, OI-013):** verify forged/expired credentials, changed organization, wrong patient, revoked caregiver delegation, withdrawn purposes, stale artifacts, direct archive bypass and mid-operation revocation using the actual HTTP/database path under test. Test fail-closed behavior with an unavailable policy provider and fresh decisions after restore. Extend the [patient authorization tests](../../apps/api/tests/test_patients.py) and [image disclosure tests](../../apps/api/tests/test_image_disclosure_authority.py). Record results, owner and independent reviewer against the exact candidate. Security/privacy and clinical reviewers must separately accept the intended use, jurisdiction-specific obligations, agreements, recovery, incident response and critical clinical-error criteria before a real-data or clinical release. Engineering checks and this policy do not establish compliance or authorization.
