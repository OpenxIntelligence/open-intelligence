# Security and governance

This is the engineering entry point for OI-004 (rights), OI-012 (intended use/data governance) and OI-013 (security/operations). The [security and governance execution plan](SECURITY_GOVERNANCE_PLAN.md) owns the control backlog. The current operating boundary is independently authored fictional records only. No identity assurance, security certification, clinical deployment, data agreement or distribution clearance follows from the synthetic application.

## Enforce authority in the existing owners

| Decision | Observable implementation | What a reviewer must try |
|---|---|---|
| Who is acting? | [`access.py`](../../apps/api/src/oi_api/access.py) defaults to deny when used through the application composition. Its opt-in fixture hashes tokens and rereads the grant on each authentication call. The bounded principal carries actor, organization, allowed patient IDs/actions, operations or research purpose and expiry. No bearer belongs in a database receipt or log. | Missing, duplicate, expired and malformed authorization; unavailable grant provider; a valid fixture for another organization or patient. Refuse disclosure; do not downgrade an unavailable authorization service to anonymous access. |
| What may that actor do now? | Canonical registry, active patient/case binding and per-action domain checks apply even to a known source/question/image ID. Source or image metadata, original bytes, evidence comparison and report download have distinct operations. | Remove only the original-read or evidence-use grant; leave metadata allowed. Revoke between preparation and the final check on paths that provide one. An accepted read receipt records prepared disclosure, not client delivery or globally atomic revocation. |
| May this source be used for this purpose? | [Rights broker](../RIGHTS_BROKER.md) and authored evidence records preserve source/content identity, permissions and withdrawal; current permitted acts can differ for metadata, content and report export. | Withdraw evidence after a comparison; confirm new use is blocked, historical provenance remains scoped, and browser history, caches or retries do not resurrect hidden content. |
| Is this effect still valid? | Receipts, immutable source versions and explicit amendment handling belong to domain owners. The EHR candidate grants `note.write`/`note.read`; it does not attest a note. A confirmed past retry and authorization for new work are separate checks. | Change a case source before a new citation, compare an ordinary manual document with a structured import, race a revocation or amendment against a write, and attempt cross-case retries. Do not adopt a model's approval or infer credentials from an author label. |
| What reaches the network? | Local Compose exposes the API on loopback; its frontend bridge allows outbound routing. Host filtering, request limits and `Cache-Control: no-store` do not enforce network egress policy. | Exercise unsupported Host, oversized input and auth failures. Inspect client error bodies, access logs, traces and build artifacts for leaked tokens or source text. The current scope gives no permission to send patient, restricted or fictional case material to a provider. |

## Before a non-demo environment

These are **required decisions and tests**, not implementations or compliance claims. Institutional identity, privacy, security, clinical and operations responsibilities must be assigned to named people before launch.

| Accountable role | Deliverable and fail-closed criterion |
|---|---|
| Identity + institutional administrator | Validate OIDC issuer, audience, signature, expiry, nonce/session policy and key rotation. Establish MFA, workforce/professional verification, patient identity recovery, organization membership and deprovisioning. Map verified identity into the existing `Authentication` port. A valid login alone grants no patient access. |
| Privacy + domain owner | Version access basis/consent, grantor, representative authority, scope, purpose, jurisdiction, expiry and revocation. Distinguish treatment, patient access, caregiver delegation and research. Specify effect on current requests, jobs, indexes, exports, downloads and restored backups. Negative cross-tenant and revoked-access tests must pass. |
| Security + operations | Design TLS, encryption at rest, least-privilege DB roles, scoped secrets, rotation and restoration of keys, allowlisted egress and isolated backups. Exercise compromise, emergency revocation, recovery and retention/deletion. A hash or fixture token is not encryption or proof of who authored a record. |
| Audit + privacy | Log minimum identifiers (who, scope, purpose, decision, operation, resource/version, time and request ID) in a protected access-history system. Preserve necessary immutable clinical history separately from observability. Define retention, tamper detection, visibility to patients and incident contacts; never log full tokens or clinical text. |
| Rights + release owners | Verify exact lockfile artifacts, transitive licenses, embedded assets, models, data, container layers, service obligations and notices; decide distribution license separately. Resolve vulnerabilities by exact artifact, disposition and recheck date. Keep unresolved components out. |

Verification starts with `python tools/audit_ci_data_boundaries.py` and the repository's [H1 API](../../.github/workflows/h1-api.yml) and [H1 web](../../.github/workflows/h1-web.yml) gates. A passing prohibited-data scan does not establish consent or certify security. Record incident/revocation and restore evidence using [the operations runbook](DEPLOYMENT_AND_OPERATIONS.md) and [release acceptance](RELEASE_ACCEPTANCE.md).
