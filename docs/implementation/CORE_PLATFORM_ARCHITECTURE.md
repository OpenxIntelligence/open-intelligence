# Core platform architecture

This is the implementation map for the connected patient application. The first-party owners below serve one patient and case across Records, Journey, Investigations, Review and follow-through. The accepted product direction and the earlier integration decisions remain in the [first-principles plan](../FIRST_PRINCIPLES_PLAN.md) and [architecture decisions](ARCHITECTURE_DECISIONS.md). The [status record](IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md) identifies which revision and environment qualify each claim.

## From intake to a patient-facing result

| Step | Implemented owner and interface | Invariant or boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Establish context | The registry, organization/purpose grant and active case binding authorize a patient route. The binding maps a qualified source organization, source system and subject. [Registry](../../src/open_intelligence/postgres_registry.py), [access](../../apps/api/src/oi_api/access.py), [bindings](../../apps/api/src/oi_api/case_bindings.py). | An identifier in a report cannot create a person or grant access. The local credential fixture supports synthetic operations. |
| Receive and version | For the supported inputs, each route passes its original to the source or document owner. New versions carry their original digest, source context and predecessor. The laboratory importer binds its command key to a digest and its amendment to the expected current predecessor. [Sources](../../apps/api/src/oi_api/sources.py), [lab custody](../../apps/api/src/oi_api/laboratory_fhir_store.py), [manual documents](../../apps/api/src/oi_api/reviewed_documents.py). | A retry recovers its own command receipt. A correction preserves the original and all historical citations. The upload, the source's own assertion and any later review are distinct facts. |
| Verify diagnostic identity | Laboratory observations use a source-version-local observation reference with its specimen, value/absence and times. Image objects link to an exact report, use qualified patient context and are identified by Study, Series and SOP Instance UIDs. [Lab profile](../../apps/api/src/oi_api/laboratory_fhir_r4.py), [image custody](../../apps/api/src/oi_api/images.py). | Hierarchy checks run while the canonical case lock is held. An ambiguous hierarchy blocks the grouped index before a disclosure receipt. A valid legacy original remains available through its separately authorized exact read. |
| Present | The registry session fetches authorized case projections. Journey filters the server-ordered laboratory timeline by exact test, report/specimen and value/absent state. Records groups linked images under their studies and series. [World model](../../apps/api/src/oi_api/source_world_model.py), [Journey](../../apps/web/src/world-model-view.tsx), [Records](../../apps/web/src/source-reading.tsx). | Filters and page selection are transient. Studies shown are limited to the selected report's links. An image index grant and an original download grant are independent. |
| Investigate, review, act | The question owner snapshots the appropriate source context. Component-specific owners attach manual passages or laboratory observation anchors, evidence links, versioned authored reports and task history. [Question anchors](../../apps/api/src/oi_api/question_anchors.py), [evidence](../../apps/api/src/oi_api/question_evidence.py), [follow-through](../../apps/api/src/oi_api/question_followthrough.py). | A source amendment changes freshness. Receipt, reading, evidence comparison and clinical attestation have distinct authority. |

## Domain ownership beyond the first diagnostic workflow

| Domain | Write owner or intended owner | Read and extension boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Organization, workforce, patient and caregiver identity, consent | Existing registry owns synthetic patient identifiers, actor grants and case binding. Qualified person identity, organization membership, caregiver delegation, consent receipt and withdrawal remain qualification work. | Enforce permitted patient, purpose and operation at retrieval and again at disclosure/commit. The future identity provider supplies identity evidence; domain owners still authorize actions. |
| Encounters and notes | The integrated candidate owns encounter revision and immutable note versions in the EHR schema. | Read or amend against the same patient and encounter. A signed clinical note requires a qualified signer, signature and amendment policy. |
| Diagnoses, disease episodes and staging | Current source-reported evidence belongs to source versions. Episode identity, diagnosis reconciliation and versioned staging rules require dedicated accountable clinical write paths. | Preserve disagreement and unassigned evidence. Staging must identify the scheme, version, licensed content and independent adjudicator. |
| Observations, pathology, imaging and genomics | The diagnostic source holds what was received. Laboratory facts and image links use typed child records. Pathology spans, biomarker interpretations, variants and lesions require separate versioned contracts. | Link back to exact report, specimen, assay/measurement context and original location. A generated interpretation is a proposal with its own identity. |
| Medication, allergy, procedure, treatment and infusion | Proposed domain command owners must distinguish a statement, reconciliation, order, administration, cancellation and toxicity record. | An authorized investigator may inspect relevant evidence. Writing orders, professional reconciliation and medication administration require their own grants and independent acceptance. |
| Investigations, evidence, reports, tasks | Existing investigation and coordination schemas own their respective source anchors, links, reports, receipts and task transitions. | Reuse their immutable keys and history in future tumor board, trial and registry modules. |
| Research, scientific outputs and audit | Existing research probes run behind separate data and rights policies. The patient registry journal records scoped platform events. | A future scientific run owns input/output manifests, reference versions and QC. A link from a research artifact to a patient requires independently granted scope. |

## Contracts for the next infrastructure increment

- **API and operational limits.** The [composition root](../../apps/api/src/oi_api/app.py) exposes the version manifest and OpenAPI at `/v1/openapi.json`. [Request limits](../../apps/api/src/oi_api/request_limits.py) apply endpoint-specific byte and time budgets. Pagination, conflict and idempotency contracts are operation-specific. The separate [command-admission PR #543](https://github.com/OpenxIntelligence/open-intelligence/pull/543) should be reconciled against the combined encounter/lab routes before changing global admission. Measure burst and sustained tenant demand before selecting distributed rate limiting.
- **Durable work.** Keep bounded imports synchronous and transactional. For the first long-running extraction or scientific job, persist an idempotent intent, immutable input hashes and grant snapshot, bounded lease, retry history and result manifest. Recheck authority when acquiring input, executing and disclosing output. Existing [run candidate #529](https://github.com/OpenxIntelligence/open-intelligence/pull/529) is a donor for deterministic source briefs. Verify crash after external work but before commit, expired lease, double claim and revocation. A broker decision follows measured backlog/latency and worker isolation requirements.
- **Artifacts.** Original bytes are currently bounded by format and stored under source ownership; the archive adapter has a separate authority contract. Larger images, raw sequencing and other scientific artifacts need a staged upload with trusted digest, completed-manifest transition, scoped retrieval, expiry, abort cleanup and backup/restore proof before external blob storage is enabled.
- **AI.** Separate clinical-source write permissions from inference, proposal storage and review. Preserve task, model, prompt, configuration, evidence references, source/rights version, abstention and human disposition. Require executable checks for unsupported or contradicted claims and temporal leakage. [AI architecture](AI_PLATFORM_ARCHITECTURE.md).
- **Operations.** Keep Compose and the exact package inputs for local synthetic integration. The application and web inventories cover scoped build artifacts. Institutional operations additionally require signed images and OS inventory, managed secrets, tested identity/consent withdrawal, observability that excludes record text, response procedures and independent release acceptance. [Deployment](DEPLOYMENT_AND_OPERATIONS.md) and [SBOM register](LICENSE_AND_SBOM_REGISTER.md).

## Where to extend diagnostic support

The [clinical model](CLINICAL_DATA_MODEL.md) records the additional work for quantitative, threshold, ordinal, narrative, coded, component, microbiology and molecular results, and for cross-sectional, projection, multi-frame, slide and other imaging profiles. For each new form, extend the importer, storage constraint, historical read, UI and failure fixtures together. An unsupported shape must fail before durable writes. Each positive path needs an original-byte round trip, amendment or relink, wrong-subject denial, revoked access and reproducible recovery.
