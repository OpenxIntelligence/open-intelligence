# Therapeutic discovery architecture

**Scope: research hypotheses only; main baseline `f188aecc70f3`, 5 November 2026.** A result may support an experiment or reveal counterevidence. Neither an in-silico score nor an experiment authorizes treatment, trial enrollment, manufacture or patient contact.

The [discovery parser](../../src/open_intelligence/discovery.py) and [rights-linked reference workflow](../DISCOVERY.md) handle fictional literature/trial items. [Crossref](../../tools/crossref_metadata.py) is a metadata-only lookup; a DOI identifies a publication but cannot establish a finding. The connected [authored-evidence](../../apps/api/src/oi_api/authored_evidence.py) and [report owners](../../apps/api/src/oi_api/evidence_report.py) provide existing evidence provenance and review surfaces. No connected target-screening, compound, docking, dynamics, ADMET, HLA or neoantigen pipeline is evidenced by these components.

## One traceable hypothesis before a screening engine

Tie a research question to an authorized project and the [scientific run manifest](BIOINFORMATICS_ARCHITECTURE.md). Identify the target by namespace, version, species, sequence/isoform or structure hash; identify a compound by exact structure, stereochemistry and preparation assumptions. Represent combination hypotheses as distinct interventions with explicit mechanism and counterevidence, not as a union of two single-agent scores. For HLA/neoantigen hypotheses, pin allele/typing method, transcript/build, variant identity, expression evidence and prediction model; an unavailable input stays unknown.

| Proposed record | Evidence the reviewer needs |
|---|---|
| Evidence item | DOI/PMID/registry ID when supplied, source/version/date, study type and model system, direction of finding, limitations, applicable rights, exact permitted passage, correction/retraction status with check date (or unknown). Preserve conflicting sources with separate links. |
| Computation | Research purpose; immutable inputs; structures/sequence/annotations and checksums; pinned workflow/environment; parameters, units, randomness, QC, uncertainty and limitations. Keep excluded/failed candidates and reasons alongside ranked results. |
| Hypothesis version | Question, explicit falsifiable prediction and alternative explanation; references to evidence and run IDs; status and accountable researcher. Record “predicted,” “observed under these experimental conditions,” “contradicted,” or “unresolved” without automatic promotion. |
| Experiment return | Versioned method, controls, conditions, result/QC artifacts, limits, actor and review event. Corrections to an input or retractions flag dependent hypotheses for reassessment; preserve the original rationale and contrary results. |

**Work sequence.** Start with two independently authored fictional sources that disagree about one target in different model systems. Demonstrate review and rights withdrawal before adding tools. Then add one isolated adapter (e.g. structure preparation or molecular comparison), test known negative controls, reproducibility and ranking sensitivity. Docking/dynamics, protein interaction or pathway analyses, drug-target/ADMET studies, combinations and immune predictions each need a separate protocol, licensed assets and domain expert acceptance. Do not substitute one score for cross-assay comparability, biological efficacy or patient benefit. No executable or weights are selected here.

**Acceptance (OI-004, OI-008, OI-016, OI-018):** reproduce an approved synthetic run and ranking; show why a candidate was excluded; display both conflicting evidence and an “unknown” retraction status; preserve lineage when correcting a publication or withdrawing display rights. Test that rights withdrawal blocks future content reads, a research identity cannot write to a chart, and a failed or stale run cannot publish a fresh hypothesis. Extend the [existing discovery rights tests](../../tests/test_discovery_rights.py) and [biological asset tests](../../tests/test_biological_assets.py) when porting contracts into H1. No clinical efficacy, external collaboration or experimental result is asserted.
