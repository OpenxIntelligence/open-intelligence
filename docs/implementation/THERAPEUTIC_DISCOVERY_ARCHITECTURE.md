# Therapeutic discovery, trials and literature architecture

**Decision at 2026-11-05: extend an existing investigation with a falsifiable research hypothesis, not a treatment recommendation.** Keep Stage 8 dependent on a reproducible Stage 7 input, a scoped research question, rights-qualified evidence and independent review. First demonstrate a calculation on invented molecules/sequences linked to an authored evidence item. Do not acquire patient material, train a model, contact a trial site, register a participant or commission an experiment.

## Keep these existing boundaries

At [baseline 299cfb5](https://github.com/OpenxIntelligence/open-intelligence/tree/299cfb54df8259c6d72ddd88f3e0c08ca3dda3f4), [November audit rows 13A–D](../project-state/oncology-audit-20261104/README.md) distinguish proposals/manual hypotheses and research probes from missing molecular design, screening and validation pipelines. [Discovery](../DISCOVERY.md) and the [rights broker](../RIGHTS_BROKER.md) are reference implementations, not grants for network access. [The Crossref adapter](../../tools/crossref_metadata.py) takes one validated public DOI, has a bounded metadata projection and an offline default. It is not case-specific literature appraisal. [The trial probe](../../tools/research/trial_screening_probe.py) separates criterion, registry and site signals; its historical public experiments are not approved patient matching.

The H1 owners for case-authored evidence, literal comparisons, rights and [immutable question reports](../engineering/H1_AUTHORED_EVIDENCE_REPORTS.md) already exist on the FastAPI/PostgreSQL side. Use those contracts, source/case authority and the amendment path. [Experimental-loop extension](../research/EXPERIMENTAL_LOOP_EXTENSION_2026-10-31.md) remains a proposal. Do not port the reference SQLite database as a second canonical service. OI-004 handles rights; OI-008 evidence; OI-009/018 corrections; OI-012/013 purpose, authority and isolation; OI-014 independent evaluation; OI-016 the specialist modality; OI-017 trial screening. None grants clinical sign-off, specimen release or a real trial workflow.

## Research record and claim separation

| Record and accountable role | Required provenance and distinction |
|---|---|
| Question/hypothesis — named investigator | Canonical question ID, case/episode if authorized, frozen source versions, data cutoff, competing hypotheses, mechanism, explicit falsifier and decision that the information could change. Unknown and counterevidence are first-class. No unsourced patient extrapolation. |
| Scientific run — pipeline owner | [Scientific run receipt](BIOINFORMATICS_ARCHITECTURE.md): exact artifact hashes, reference releases, container digest, tool version, defaults, seed, QC, environment, outputs, failures and time. Docking adds receptor structure/model provenance, chains/assembly, ligand protonation, stereo, docking box and scoring method. Dynamics adds force field, solvent, timestep, thermodynamic ensemble, sampling/restarts and platform precision. |
| Hypothesis evidence — evidence owner | Versioned graph edge “observed”, “computed”, “predicted”, “experimentally supported in specified system”, “contradicted”, or “insufficient”. Capture organism, tissue/model, assay, endpoint, comparator, evidence grade, effect **with units** and uncertainty; cited version, source span and use permissions. Duplicate database assertions or models trained on the same evidence are not independent confirmations. |
| Review — named scientific reviewer | Inspect uncertainty, method applicability, controls, conflicts, failed experiments, alternative explanation and conflicts of interest. Sign a scoped scientific review on the specific snapshot. A technical QC review cannot promote a candidate into the chart, order set, medication list or patient explanation of treatment benefit. |
| Follow-up — investigation owner | Record which controlled experiment or evidence would discriminate candidates, who could review it, and why. Any real tissue transfer, wet-lab work, trial contact or clinical action has its own authority and qualified accountable operator. |

**Useful first question:** On a tiny independently authored set with two records of the same invented structure and two stereoisomers, does a deterministic standardization rule collapse only the intended duplicate? Keep submitted structures plus standardized forms. Record explicit stereochemistry/salt/tautomer policy, valence errors and ambiguity. This demonstrates identity/lineage, not targeting, efficacy or drug suitability.

**Drug, target and combination evidence.** Preserve chemical and target identifiers, species/isoform, assay, experimental conditions, activity units and operator (e.g. “>” vs exact IC50), replicate count and relation to independently measured data. Component and combination results have different evidence scopes: an observed effect on two individual targets does not establish combination synergy. A proposed assay requires a concentration matrix, independent controls, prespecified interaction model, uncertainty and stopping criteria. Do not compute a patient ranking from disconnected in-vitro results. [ChEMBL](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chembldb/) advertises CC BY-SA 3.0 for its database: before considering an adapter, select the release, review attribution/share-alike and record any cited-paper/assay rights separately. No records were acquired.

**Selection gate.** Scientific dependencies stay in independently built, locked workers; evaluate a direct deterministic baseline first. Test repeatability, input-loss round trips, peak RSS/runtime, reproducible environment build and offline execution at a fixed fixture size. For molecular dynamics and predictors test platform/seed sensitivity; architectural candidates were not performance-benchmarked here. Upstream release records for [RDKit](https://github.com/rdkit/rdkit/releases/tag/Release_2026_09_2), [Biotite](https://github.com/biotite-dev/biotite/releases/tag/v2.0.0), [Mol*](https://github.com/molstar/molstar/releases/tag/v5.13.0) and [Vina](https://github.com/forlilab/AutoDock-Vina/releases/tag/v1.2.7) establish release history, not continuing support, compatibility or clinical validation. Require exact SBOM/NOTICEs, dependency/asset review, rescanning, bounded compute and independent reproduction before any integration.

## Versioning, deduplication and rights

The evidence catalog should store a provider-specific record version and hash, stable DOI/PMID/PMCID/NCT IDs when present, article version (preprint versus journal), publication and update dates, retrieval timestamp, search protocol/cutoff, type of study, status of retraction/correction **as of a recorded check**, and rights by item/use. Store “unknown/not checked” distinctly from “no signal as of date”. Track trial status and site timestamp independently. Repeated status checks with unchanged payload do not create new study results.

Normalize identifiers conservatively; an exact DOI or a source-declared PMID–DOI mapping can link versions, but distinct publications from the same clinical study remain distinct records. Fuzzy title/author/year matches create a proposed duplicate pair, not an automatic merge. Emit conflicting identifiers for manual review. A linked retraction notice is a separate object, not an overwrite of the paper. Missing DOI/PMID is allowed if other source identity exists. Group reports from the same underlying trial to avoid multiplicity in synthesis.

Rights gates precede storage, display, quotations, redistribution, embedding/training and provider use. Check both original and current rights at export. Start with citation-only or the exact metadata whitelist. Full-text appraisal requires a legally usable version and an exact passage/figure anchor; study findings must name population, design, intervention/exposure, comparator, outcome, direction/magnitude and uncertainty where available. A human records support, contradiction, context or unknown; evaluates risk of bias, applicability, funding and independence; preserves discordant findings. Metadata-only records may support discovery, never an efficacy finding. When an input, study status or permission changes, stale only the linked assessment; do not silently rewrite its signed status or leak content hidden by new restrictions.
