## One product shell, three levels

1. **Application context:** patient, caregiver, clinician or researcher *only when authorized by server-owned identity/relationship policy*; otherwise label a layout preference as presentation.
2. **Selected-patient workspace:** persistent authorized patient/case context with Overview, Records/Documents, Journey/World Model, Investigations, Evidence, Review and Coordination *only as they become connected*. Empty, denied, unknown and not-connected states must remain distinguishable.
3. **Task and specialist context:** original-page/line inspection, source amendment and comparison, investigation/question evidence, accountable review, and eventually pathology/imaging/genomics/laboratory/oncology/trial-specific views. Do not turn every service into a noisy permanent primary navigation entry.

All levels must use one canonical patient identity, server-enforced permissions and source-owned Patient World Model/domain records. `RegistrySession` is a transient client adapter, **not** a new authorization, patient or evidence ledger. H1 API routes already map identity reads, case bindings, exact source versions/originals, World Model, questions, read-only verification packets, review queues/returns and follow-through to independently checked server owners. Client route labels do not create capabilities.
