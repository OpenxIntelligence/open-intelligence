# Open Intelligence — Complementary Capability Competitive Due Diligence
**31 October 2026 · Supplemental competitive research for the existing project; not a strategic replacement, launch decision or certified market survey**

## Adversarial design filters for incremental capabilities
- A capability implemented mostly inside an EHR is not a durable independent company. EHRs can add chart summaries, reasoning, missingness alerts, order/plan tracking and clinical copilots.
- Drug companies can build response models, wet labs, assays, closed-loop discovery and internal therapeutic development. “AI biotech” alone has no structural advantage.
- AI-agent authorization is being standardized and sold by identity/security vendors. Governance primitives alone do not create healthcare distribution.
- A beautiful patient model / molecular profiling / trial list / genomics report is necessary **infrastructure**, not evidence of demand or moat.
- Strong differentiation must be *independent patient-side continuity, future option dependencies, coordination across competing institutions, measured execution, and evidence of earlier useful action*. Even these remain unproven.

## Competitor clusters to verify afresh before positioning
| Cluster | Examples | Overlap to test | Potential remaining gap (hypothesis only) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Longitudinal precision-oncology/data | Tempus, Caris, Flatiron, xCures | clinical/omics patient state; temporal evidence; source-linked data; models | independent across vendor systems; practical option preservation/execution |
| Molecular diagnostic vendors | Foundation Medicine, Guardant, Natera | biomarkers, therapies and trial data derived from their tests | cross-vendor raw assets and unresolved specimen/tissue dependencies |
| Oncology guidance/second opinions | Cancer Commons, CureMatch, Genomate, Nxtcure | treatment possibilities, expert guidance, genomics-informed decisions | portfolio deadline/critical-path management and execution receipts |
| Trial/access navigation | Massive Bio, myTomorrows | screening, referral, navigation, access, coordination | integrate trials with tissue, future therapy sequencing, experimental buildability and record lineage |
| EHR/platform | Epic, Oracle Health | longitudinal records, tasks, referrals, clinician workflows | patient-authorized cross-institutional research assets and long-term continuity |
| Clinical research/therapeutic development | pharma, CROs, specialized labs/manufacturers | experiments, option development, sample processing, manufacturing | neutral patient-side orchestration; incumbent can still partner or compete |

These are **adversarial hypotheses**. A broad feature list proves neither competitive absence nor unique rights to data. Verify exact present-day product, customer segment, partner network, claims, active deployments, pricing, data portability, regulatory status, and defensible claims by dated primary sources and expert calls.

## Evidence-led comparative tests
1. Request a real product demo or detailed documentation for **the same fictional P-001 case** from competing vendors.
2. Ask each: can the product show tissue remaining, inaccessible raw data, trial criterion expiry, lead times, historic belief states, linked contradictory findings, and executed preparation receipts?
3. Compare total work and outcomes versus a staffed oncology navigator using ordinary EHR/Docs/task software.
4. Explore who would own the patient relationship, consent, original data portability, specimen release authority and service contracts.
5. Test whether the buyer would pay *for saved preparation time and preserved actionable options* rather than for another summary.

## Evidence/rights boundaries
- Do not embed vendor trademarks, licensed charts, proprietary source code, datasets, private patient materials, paywalled guideline text or clinical decision algorithms in repo.
- Public claims about competitor capabilities must carry dated authoritative links and be refreshed before a fundraising document or external claim.
- Avoid describing the public story of Sid Sijbrandij as efficacy proof. His public account is a motivating illustration of complex coordination, not a controlled causal trial.
- No guarantee of “zero legal risk.” Rights and clinical governance are independently gated.

## Diligence decision
**Evaluate as a complementary Open Intelligence workflow, not as a replacement company thesis.** Evidence-backed option dependencies and human-authorized follow-through may add value to the existing platform, but differentiation, willingness to pay and market demand are not established. Failure of this optional workflow does not invalidate the existing architecture or research agenda.
