# IV-04 — roles, conflicts and independence

**Blank assessment instructions, not a completed reviewer assessment.** Apply to one claim and one frozen evaluation. Use the existing IV-02 rubric and IV-03 baseline procedures; do not create another clinical scoring system here.

## Classify the contribution before making a claim

| Role | Contribution | Claim that must not follow automatically |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Independent evaluator | Challenges a specified claim using agreed inputs and criteria, with recorded independence assessment | Independent clinical validation merely because a reviewer has a professional title |
| Co-developer / feature author | Designs or implements the feature, fixture, reference key or adaptation being tested | Independent confirmation of that same work |
| Clinical adviser | Defines meaningful questions, plausibility and limitations | Institutional endorsement or an independently executed evaluation |
| Patient advocate / human-factors contributor | Reviews language, burden, usability and uncertainty from the relevant perspective | Clinical efficacy judgment, representative patient consensus or authorization to collect health data |
| Experimental collaborator | Proposes or, under a separate authorized protocol, executes an experimental study | Permission to move specimens or infer human benefit from an experimental observation |
| Coordinator / adjudicator | Manages access and discrepancies or resolves disputed judgments | Authority to erase dissent, hide failed runs or approve work outside their recorded remit |

One person can have multiple disclosed roles. Assess independence **per claim and task**, not as a universal label. A person who advised design can provide useful formative feedback; describe it as such. When independence cannot be established, assign another assessor, limit the claim, or keep the outcome unadjudicated.

## Questions to record privately

Record `yes`, `no`, `unknown`, or `not_applicable`, supporting explanation, date and assessment owner. An unknown answer is unresolved; it is not evidence of independence.

1. Did the proposed assessor implement or tune the feature under evaluation?
2. Did they author, select or revise the evaluated cases, reference answers, thresholds or scoring criteria?
3. Have they seen the public reference key, future cutoff inputs, previous arm outputs or results for these cases? Which items and when?
4. Is there employment, funding, equity, payment, authorship, supervisory or other involvement relevant to this claim? What mitigation is proposed?
5. Does the assessor have the domain expertise required for this question, and what evidence supports that fit? Do not infer qualification from a job title alone.
6. Can they record negative, null and inconclusive findings without outcome-contingent compensation or required favorable wording?
7. Will the assessor's institution permit the proposed activity and their representation? Do not assume an individual speaks for an institution.
8. Who adjudicates disagreement, what conflicts does that person have, and how will unresolved dissent remain visible?
9. Is the proposed role developmental critique, independent technical assessment, clinical reference adjudication or future experiment? Which is **not** being claimed?

Store identities, contact details, conflict explanations and private correspondence only in an authorized restricted register. Public artifacts may use permitted de-identified reviewer references; no private register rows are supplied in this package.

## Exposure and assignment rules

The existing public IV-01 cases and designer key are practice material. Removing answers from an export does not restore blinding for someone who has already seen them. Use practice material for familiarization; keep familiarization results separate from any confirmatory result. Fresh evaluation cases require separately controlled access, independent reference preparation, defined cutoffs and exposure records before assessment.

For a paired manual/simple/application comparison, adopt the reviewed baseline and arm-order plan from IV-03. Do not let the same reviewer first see a complete case and then claim ignorance of later facts during earlier-cutoff scoring. Changes to assignments or inputs after exposure require a recorded deviation and potentially a new evaluation, not retrospective relabeling.

## Disagreement, recusal and record retention

Keep original observations, each assessor's judgment, source/version/anchor, rationale and adjudication result. Never replace disagreement with a fabricated consensus. Record a recusal without requiring public disclosure of sensitive reasons. The activity owner decides how compensation, publication, confidentiality and conflicts are handled before participation; this template supplies no contract terms or legal determination.

**Output:** a private claim-specific role/independence decision, unresolved items, and permitted scope. A signed-looking text field is not authentication or proof of independent evaluation. No assessments have been completed by writing this document.
